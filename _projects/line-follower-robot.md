### Overview

The Line Follower Robot was an introductory robotics project
focused on developing foundational skills in electronics,
programming, system design, and robotic assembly.

The project covered the development process from initial design
and simulation through programming, assembly, and maintenance.

### Development Process

The project involved several stages:

- Designing and simulating the robotic system.
- Developing the required programming.
- Performing electronic assembly and wiring.
- Testing and maintaining the completed system.

### Highlights

- Designed and simulated the line-following robotic system.
- Applied fundamental electronics and system design concepts.
- Performed electronic assembly and wiring.
- Developed the programming required for the robot.
- Gained hands-on experience with system maintenance.
